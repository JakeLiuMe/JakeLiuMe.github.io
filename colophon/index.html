Colophon

How this site is made.

Form
Static HTML and CSS, with a short script for the performance charts (range toggle, crosshair, weekly bars), a copy button, the menu, and entrance motion. The charts need the script; the rest of every page reads with the script blocked. Public daily figures live in data/daily.json. A ledger-integrity workbook and a daily-P&L notebook sit beside them in /data/.
Type
Source Serif 4, Inter, and IBM Plex Mono, self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License.
Palette
Ink Paper Oxblood
Third parties
None. No trackers, cookies, or remote scripts. Fonts and pages are served from this domain.
Host
GitHub Pages, on the custom domain jakeliu.me.

A figure on this site comes with its source, its date, and the limit of what it measures.
