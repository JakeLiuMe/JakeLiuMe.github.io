The report borrows its form from annual reports and financial statements: hairline rules, tabular figures, segment reviews and notes to the accounts. The point is that every claim can be checked.

The site is static HTML and CSS, generated from plain-text files by a short Python script and served by GitHub Pages. There are no trackers, cookies or third-party scripts. Charts are drawn when the site is built, from public data such as npm’s download counts and from my own records, and each one has a data table underneath.

I built it with AI coding agents under the same rules as the rest of my work. Every number traces to a note, the agents work from a list of claims they may not make, and the publishing step leaves out anything tagged as waiting on an approval.
