Process and risk

Six steps, then size.

The order I use to take a strategy forward. Backtests stay off this page. The record above is settled live P&L only.

  1. 01

    Hypothesis

    A written claim, and the observation that would retire it, before any code exists.

  2. 02

    Backtest

    History decides whether the idea earns a harder test. A pass here is permission to continue. It is not a result I publish.

  3. 03

    Permutation test

    The result has to stand apart from a shuffled baseline. An options project returned p = 0.995 on this kind of test. I read that as overfitting and closed it before risking capital. A strategy that fails here does not go live.

  4. 04

    Shadow

    The strategy runs against live markets with no capital. Shadow results stay out of the record above.

  5. 05

    Small live

    Size starts small. The test is whether the live fills match the specification I wrote.

  6. 06

    Scale

    Size was scaled in stages. The record above is what traded live.
